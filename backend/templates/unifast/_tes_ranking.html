TES — Rule-Based Ranking

Only students whose TES application is still awaiting a decision appear here, and only those whose record is complete enough to rank. An approved or rejected application has already been decided and is not ranked. {{ tes_applicant_total }} pending applicant{{ tes_applicant_total|pluralize }} screened.

Order, applied in this sequence:

  1. Priority group — Priority 1 before Priority 2. Priority 1 is a Listahanan-listed or 4Ps household; Priority 2 is everyone else who qualifies.
  2. Household per capita income, lowest first — annual family income divided by household size.
  3. Number of priority markers, most first — PWD, solo-parent dependent, indigenous community member.
  4. Surname, alphabetically, to break any remaining tie.

Open Why? on any row for the rule-by-rule reason behind its verdict. Not Eligible students are excluded from both lists; their count is in the summary cards above.
