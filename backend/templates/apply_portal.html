About the BiPSU Staff Scholarship

The BiPSU Staff Scholarship is an institutional benefit granted by Biliran Province State University (BiPSU) to its permanent faculty and employees, as well as their qualified dependents. It was established to recognize the dedication of university personnel and to support their families in pursuing higher education within the institution.

This scholarship covers tuition and miscellaneous fees for one academic year, renewable each semester provided the scholar maintains good academic standing.

About the Affirmative Action Scholarship

The Affirmative Action Scholarship at Biliran Province State University (BiPSU) is designed to promote inclusivity and equal access to quality education. It targets students who have demonstrated academic potential in Senior High School and performed well in the university's admission examination.

This scholarship aims to provide opportunities for deserving students who may not qualify for traditional merit-based programs but show strong potential for academic success.
