TES — Rule-Based Recommendation

Download list

Nobody applies for TES here, so every student on file is screened — {{ tes_student_total }} in all. UniFAST awards TES; this list is what the SDSO would recommend, and no status is written onto any student by producing it.

Order, applied in this sequence:

  1. Priority group — Priority 1 before Priority 2. Priority 1 is a Listahanan-listed or 4Ps household; Priority 2 is everyone else who qualifies.
  2. Household per capita income, lowest first — annual family income divided by household size.
  3. Number of priority markers, most first — PWD, solo-parent dependent, indigenous community member.
  4. Surname, alphabetically, to break any remaining tie.

Open Why? on any row for the rule-by-rule reason behind its verdict. Students whose record cannot answer a rule are held out into the list below rather than ranked on what is missing.
