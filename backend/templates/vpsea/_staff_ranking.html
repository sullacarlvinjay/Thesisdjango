A specialized program of the University giving full privilege to members of the faculty and staff and their legitimate dependents, as approved by the Board of Regents. {{ staff_total }} application{{ staff_total|pluralize }} screened.

The qualifications, as approved:

  1. (a) All faculty and employees of the University with permanent appointment.
  2. (b) All qualified and legitimate dependents of faculty and staff with permanent appointment.
  3. (c) Staff dependents who have already graduated a baccalaureate degree are disqualified from enjoying the scholarship.

Nothing is scored — the programme has no merit test, so this states a verdict and its reason rather than a position. Open Why? on any row for the qualification-by-qualification reading behind it.
