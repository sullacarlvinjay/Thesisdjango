How somebody reaches this list

The qualifications, as approved by the Board of Regents:

  1. (a) All faculty and employees of the University with permanent appointment.
  2. (b) All qualified and legitimate dependents of faculty and staff with permanent appointment.
  3. (c) Staff dependents who have already graduated a baccalaureate degree are disqualified from enjoying the scholarship.

An employee is on this list for holding a permanent appointment, whether or not they have applied: the appointment and its paper are recorded when they register, and that is the whole question the programme asks of them. A dependent reaches it only by applying, because the relationship, the employee they hold it through and whether they already graduated exist nowhere else.

The Applied column separates the two: who asked for the scholarship, and who is merely eligible for it. Open Why? on any row for the qualification-by-qualification reading behind it. Anyone not eligible, and anyone the rules cannot read yet, is kept below with their reason rather than dropped.
