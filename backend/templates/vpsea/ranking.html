Every enrolled student on file is screened — students are not shown this evaluation, and producing it writes no status onto anybody.

Order, applied in this sequence:

  1. Target group — the four the programme is for: indigenous groups, persons with disabilities, students from public schools, students from depressed areas. Most groups first.
  2. Fit score, highest first — 50% SHS GPA + 50% admission exam. A tie-break between students the mandate reaches equally.
  3. Surname, alphabetically, to break any remaining tie.

This is not a merit list. Grades are not the only factor, and a qualifier need be neither indigent nor an excellent performer — PASUC-8 proposal, §1. The groups order the shortlist; they never gate it.
