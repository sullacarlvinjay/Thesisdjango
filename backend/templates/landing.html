Frequently asked questions

How registration and scholarship records work in this portal.

Who can register for an account?

BiPSU students and BiPSU faculty or staff. Students register with their student ID; staff register with their employee ID and unit. Each account belongs to one person and one email address.

I registered. Why can I not sign in yet?

Two things have to happen first. Open the confirmation link sent to the email address you registered with, then wait for the Student Development and Services Office to verify the account. You will be emailed once it is approved. Until then the sign-in page will tell you the account is still waiting.

Do I apply for a scholarship on this site?

For internal and institutional programs, yes — apply from your portal once your account is approved. External programs are funded and decided by the agency that runs them; this portal keeps the BiPSU record of who holds them.

How do I know when applications are open?

Each program has its own application window, and a closed program will say so when you try to apply. Put your email in the alerts box below and you will be written to when a window opens.

I already hold a scholarship. Do I still register?

Yes. Declare it while registering — there is a scholarship section on the form for the program, the awarding agency and your certificate. The office checks the declaration and links the record to your account.

What happens to the information I enter?

It becomes the scholarship record the office keeps, and it may be shared with the agency funding a scholarship you are awarded. The full Terms of Use and Data Privacy Notice is on the registration form and you have to accept it before an account is created.
